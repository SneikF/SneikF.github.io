During my degree I learned that reading a proof and understanding it are different things. You can nod along line by line and, once the book is closed, be unable to rebuild any of it.

What works for me is writing. When I try to explain an idea to an imaginary reader, the gaps show up: the step I took for granted, the hypothesis I can't tell where it's used.

> If I can't write it clearly, I don't understand it yet.

This blog is partly that: a place to make myself finish my ideas. -->
