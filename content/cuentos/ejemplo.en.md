My grandmother had a notebook that never ran out of pages. She used it for grocery bills, recipes and, every now and then, for writing letters to people who were no longer around.

When she died, it came to me. I counted the pages: ninety-six. The next day there were ninety-seven. The new one was blank, except for a line at the bottom in her slanted handwriting: *"Your turn."*

I didn't know what to write. I put down the date, the price of bread, and that it had rained. That night I dreamed of her kitchen.

---

Years have passed. The notebook no longer fits in the drawer. Sometimes I open it just to see whether a new page still shows up in the morning.

It always does.
