From my room I can see a window in the building across the street. Every night at eleven, someone turns on a yellow lamp and sits down to write.

I have never seen their face. Only the hand moving back and forth over the paper, and a cup that sometimes steams.

Last night the lamp didn't come on. I waited until one.

Today, in my mailbox, there was an envelope with no return address. Inside, a sheet in tight handwriting that began: *"From my room I can see a window in the building across the street…"*
