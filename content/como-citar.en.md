## What you can do

Copy, share, print, translate or adapt my texts and photos, in any medium, even for commercial purposes.

## Under two conditions

1. **Give me credit.** Name the author (Esneider Sierra), link to the work or to this blog, link to the license and say whether you made changes.
2. **Share alike.** If you modify or build on a work of mine, your version must be released under the same license (CC BY-SA 4.0).

## About the signature on the photos

Downloaded photos carry a small signature in one corner. Please don't crop it or remove it. If for some reason you do, you still have to give credit as in the examples.
